# Taste

## UI & design craft
- Expects exceptional UI polish on work they ship — asks for "perfect UI", "amazing feels/taste", meaning micro-interaction details, press/hover states, transitions, and visual consistency are treated as requirements, not nice-to-haves. Confidence: 0.75
- For UI work, wants an audit that names concrete defects with before/after and a severity/suggestion, rather than generic style advice. Confidence: 0.6

## Communication & workflow
- Writes very terse, shorthand, typo-heavy instructions and expects the agent to infer intent and keep working autonomously (e.g. "contnite form here" = resume where you left off, no recap or re-asking). Confidence: 0.7
- Builds and publishes libraries/packages (npm, scoped org) — deliverables are expected to hold up as a consumer-facing API, not just a demo. Confidence: 0.6
